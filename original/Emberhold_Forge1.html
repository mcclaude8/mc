The Smithy ofEmberhold Forge

Smelt ore, pour molds, hammer blades into shape and set gems by hand. Every weapon you forge goes to a real soldier, and they march out to fight with it.

Controls
  • WASD walk · mouse to look · E or click to use a station
  • The station bar along the bottom takes you straight to any station
  • At a station, follow the instruction at the top. Space works as the action key
  • On touch screens: drag the left side to walk and the right side to look
